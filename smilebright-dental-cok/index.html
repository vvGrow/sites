FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you treat children?

Yes, we have pediatric expertise and child-friendly setups designed to make first dental visits comfortable.

Are the procedures actually painless?

For most treatments, yes. We use topical numbing gels, modern anaesthesia, and gentle techniques so patients feel little to no discomfort.

Do you offer online consultations?

Yes — quick concerns can be addressed over WhatsApp video. For diagnosis, we always recommend an in-person visit.

Is the clinic sterilized between patients?

Absolutely. All instruments are autoclaved, disposables are single-use, and the chair is sanitized after every patient.

Can I get a treatment plan before committing?

Yes, every patient gets a detailed written treatment plan with all costs before any work begins. No pressure to proceed.

How long does an appointment take?

A check-up takes about 30 minutes. Cleaning is 45–60 minutes. We share an exact estimate when we book your slot.
