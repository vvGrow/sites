Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer guarantees on your yoga practice?

Yes. Every job comes with a service guarantee. If something is not right, we make it right at no extra cost.

Are walk-ins accepted at Trivandrum Yoga Ashram?

Yes, but we strongly recommend booking via WhatsApp first to avoid waiting time, especially for first-time visits.

What are your timings?

We are open Monday to Sunday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Sunday we close earlier, around 6:00 PM. Late visits possible by appointment.

Why should I choose Trivandrum Yoga Ashram over others in Thiruvananthapuram?

Classical practice, gentle pace. Certified yoga teachers, authentic indian tradition, and a no-pressure approach you will appreciate.

What does yoga studio consultation cost at Trivandrum Yoga Ashram?

Our initial consultation is either free or starts from a small fee. Free trial class - bring a mat.

Do you offer a free consultation or quote?

Yes — initial consultations and quotes are free. You only pay if you decide to go ahead with the service.
