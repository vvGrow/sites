Frequently Asked Questions

What are your timings?

We are open Monday to Sunday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Sunday we close earlier, around 6:00 PM. Late visits possible by appointment.

What does general clinic consultation cost at Delhi Family Clinic?

Our initial consultation is either free or starts from a small fee. Same-day appointments available.

Do you offer a free consultation or quote?

Yes — initial consultations and quotes are free. You only pay if you decide to go ahead with the service.

Do you handle a fever in the family?

Absolutely. We routinely handle a fever in the family and a long-term health concern with the same care and attention.

Do you offer home service in Delhi?

For applicable services, yes — typically within 5 km of our location. Call or WhatsApp to confirm coverage and any travel charges.

Do you offer guarantees on your family medicine?

Yes. Every job comes with a service guarantee. If something is not right, we make it right at no extra cost.
