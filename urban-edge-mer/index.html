FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your timings?

We are open Monday to Sunday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Sunday we close earlier, around 6:00 PM. Late visits possible by appointment.

What does beauty salon consultation cost at Urban Edge Salon?

Our initial consultation is either free or starts from a small fee. Walk-ins welcome - or book ahead.

Do you accept UPI / cards / cash?

Yes — cash, all UPI apps, credit and debit cards, and major wallets are accepted. Pay how you like.

How do I get a quote for beauty services?

WhatsApp us with your specific requirement (photos help). We respond with a clear written quote within minutes.

Are walk-ins accepted at Urban Edge Salon?

Yes, but we strongly recommend booking via WhatsApp first to avoid waiting time, especially for first-time visits.

Do you handle a wedding day look?

Absolutely. We routinely handle a wedding day look and a regular hair refresh with the same care and attention.
