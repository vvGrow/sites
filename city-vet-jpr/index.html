FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does veterinary clinic consultation cost at City Vet Hospital?

Our initial consultation is either free or starts from a small fee. 24/7 emergency line for pets.

Do you handle a routine vaccination?

Absolutely. We routinely handle a routine vaccination and an emergency at midnight with the same care and attention.

Are walk-ins accepted at City Vet Hospital?

Yes, but we strongly recommend booking via WhatsApp first to avoid waiting time, especially for first-time visits.

How do I get a quote for pet healthcare?

WhatsApp us with your specific requirement (photos help). We respond with a clear written quote within minutes.

Do you offer guarantees on your pet healthcare?

Yes. Every job comes with a service guarantee. If something is not right, we make it right at no extra cost.

Can I book an appointment online?

Yes. The fastest way is to WhatsApp us with your name and preferred time. We will confirm within minutes.
